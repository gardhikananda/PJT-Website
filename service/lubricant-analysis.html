Lubricant Analysis

Lubricants play an important role in reducing friction, controlling wear, dissipating heat, and protecting equipment components during operation. Changes in lubricant condition can affect its performance and may indicate contamination, degradation, or abnormal wear within the equipment.

Our Lubricant Analysis service provides laboratory testing to evaluate the condition and characteristics of lubricants used in industrial equipment. The analysis can help monitor lubricant performance and identify changes that may require further investigation or maintenance attention.

By providing clear and reliable analytical results, lubricant testing supports condition monitoring, preventive maintenance, and equipment reliability. It can also help clients make informed decisions regarding lubricant replacement, maintenance intervals, and potential equipment issues.

Scope Of Analysis

  • Fresh Oil Analysis
  • Used Oil Analysis
  • Compressor Oil Analysis
  • Engine Oil Analysis
  • Hydraulic Oil Analysis

    • Frequently asked questions

    Lubricant analysis can provide information about oil condition, contamination, and wear-related characteristics. These results can help identify changes in lubricant condition and provide indications that may require further investigation.
    Fresh Oil Analysis evaluates the initial condition and characteristics of unused lubricant, while Used Oil Analysis focuses on changes that occur during equipment operation, including contamination and wear-related indicators.
    Testing frequency depends on equipment type, operating conditions, lubricant type, criticality, and maintenance strategy. For critical equipment, periodic analysis can help establish a consistent condition-monitoring program.
