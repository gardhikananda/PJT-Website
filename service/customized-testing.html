Customized Testing

A Testing Approach Designed Around Your Needs

Different equipment, materials, and operational conditions may require different testing approaches. When standard testing services do not fully address a specific requirement, customized testing can provide a more suitable solution.

Our Customized Testing service is designed to support clients with specific analytical requirements across oils, fuels, lubricants, and other applicable samples. Testing can be discussed and tailored based on the sample type, testing objective, required parameters, and intended application.

Before testing is conducted, our team can discuss the requirement and help identify an appropriate testing approach. The resulting analysis is presented through clear laboratory reporting to support evaluation, troubleshooting, quality control, or maintenance decisions.

Scope Of Analysis

  • Specialized Oil Testing

    • Customized testing for transformer oils, lubricants, and other applicable oil-based samples according to specific analytical requirements and testing objectives.

  • Fuel Testing

    • Tailored fuel testing designed around specific fuel types, quality requirements, and analytical objectives beyond standard testing packages.

  • Equipment Condition Assessment

    • Testing solutions designed to provide additional analytical information when investigating equipment condition, abnormal results, contamination, or potential operational issues.

  • Specific Parameter Testing

    • Individual or selected testing parameters can be requested according to specific quality control, troubleshooting, monitoring, or technical requirements.

  • Customized Laboratory Solutions

    • Flexible testing arrangements for unique samples, applications, or analytical requirements that may not be covered by standard testing services.
