Company Profile

Independent Laboratory For Oils and Transformers

Prabu Jaya Tunggal is an independent laboratory specializing in oil analysis and transformer testing services for industrial applications. We provide analytical solutions designed to help businesses monitor equipment condition, identify potential problems, and make informed maintenance decisions.

Our services cover transformer oil, fuel, lubricant, and air quality testing, supported by professional laboratory practices and technical expertise. We also provide customized testing solutions to address specific requirements beyond standard testing parameters.

We understand that reliable laboratory results play an important role in maintaining equipment performance and operational continuity. That is why we are committed to delivering accurate analysis, clear reporting, and responsive technical support.
